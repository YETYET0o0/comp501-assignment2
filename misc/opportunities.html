Technology Opportunities

Opportunity 1: Improving Staff and Customer Safety

One of the most promising applications of Facial Recognition Technology (FRT) is enhancing the safety of both staff and customers at retail stores. When linked with Closed-Circuit Television (CCTV), FRT has the capability of identifying an individual who is on a security watch list. As soon as an image is matched against an individual's identity on a security watch list, it sends an alert to the retailer's staff. This potentially allows retailers to take action sooner in response to potential threats thereby reducing their exposure to future risks of harm.
