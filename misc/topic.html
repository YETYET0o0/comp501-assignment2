Retail Facial Recognition and CCTV under privacy act, in New Zealand

"Yes, you are being watched, even if no one is looking for you" (Krapp, 2022)

In today’s society, Closed-Circuit Television, so-called CCTV, is present almost everywhere people go: workplaces, train stations, shopping malls, restaurants, even universities where most people spend their days. In particular, the use of facial recognition technology and CCTV in retail environments, such as grocery stores and big-box stores, is increasing rapidly.

These technologies help deter crime, such as theft, while improving operational safety and security. they provide retailers with real-time monitoring, recorded evidence, and the ability to identify potential serucity threats more efficiently.

ex. Martin, C. D., Huff, C., Gotterbarn, D., & Miller, K. (1996). A framework for implementing and teaching the social and ethical impact of computing. Education and Information Technologies, 1(2), 101-122.
